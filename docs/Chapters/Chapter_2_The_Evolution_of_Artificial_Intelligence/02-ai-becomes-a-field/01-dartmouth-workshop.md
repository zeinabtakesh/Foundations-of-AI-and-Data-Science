# The Dartmouth Workshop

<div class="chapter-intro" markdown>

**1956 · AI becomes a field**

The Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence brought researchers together to ask whether learning, reasoning, and other aspects of intelligence could be described precisely enough for machines to reproduce them. The 1955 proposal gave this shared ambition a name: *artificial intelligence*.

Organized by [John McCarthy](#john-mccarthy){ data-open-details }, [Marvin Minsky](#marvin-minsky){ data-open-details }, [Nathaniel Rochester](#nathaniel-rochester){ data-open-details }, and [Claude Shannon](#claude-shannon){ data-open-details }, the project is widely treated as a starting point for AI as a named research field. It did not solve machine intelligence in one summer; it brought questions about language, problem solving, learning, and abstraction into a common research agenda.
